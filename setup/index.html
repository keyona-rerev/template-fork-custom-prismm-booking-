Booking Engine Setup

This saves directly into your booking page's configuration. No code, nothing else to touch.

How this works

  1. Share your calendars first. Every calendar below needs to be shared with one email before this page can read it — click the ⓘ next to any email field for exact steps.
  2. Fill in the point of contact. That's whoever must be free for a time to show up at all — bookings get written directly onto their calendar.
  3. Add everyone else who could join. At least one of them must also be free, checked top to bottom.
  4. Set your booking hours and page details.
  5. Click Save. Your booking page updates immediately — no waiting, nothing else to deploy.

Before you start: share your calendars

This page can only read a calendar that's been shared with it. Do this for every email you enter below — click the ⓘ next to any email field for exact steps.

Who's the point of contact?

This is the person whose calendar decides whether a time slot exists at all. It must be free for a slot to show up, and the booking gets written directly onto this calendar.

Share at "Make changes to events"
  1. Open Google Calendar, signed in as this person.
  2. Settings → click their calendar under "My calendars".
  3. "Share with specific people or groups" → Add people.
  1. Set permission to Make changes to events (not just free/busy — bookings write straight onto this calendar).
  2. Send.

Who else can join the call?

At least one of these people must also be free — checked in the order listed, top choice first. Their names never appear anywhere on the booking page — only on the calendar invite once someone is assigned.

When can people book?

Applies to the point of contact's calendar. Times are in the timezone below.
